Privacy Policy

Last updated · November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Radhe Krishna: Aarti & Bhajan application (the "App") handles information when you use its devotional features.

We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about the information processed through the App and its integrated third-party services.

01. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, password or home address to access its devotional content.

We do not ask you to upload personal photos or files as a condition of using the App. Features such as sharing or saving devotional images may involve images and applications that you choose on your device.

Third-party services integrated into the App may automatically process technical, usage, advertising and diagnostic information. Depending on the service, this may include:

  • Device and application information.
  • App interaction and usage information.
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction information.
  • IP address and network information.

02. Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand application usage and improve functionality, and Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and stability issues.

Depending on device settings and SDK behaviour, these services may process app interactions, device and app information, crash logs, diagnostic data and identifiers.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

03. Advertising – Google AdMob

The App may display advertisements through Google AdMob in production versions of the application.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information to provide, measure and improve advertisements. Depending on the advertising configuration, consent choices and applicable regional requirements, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers, where available.
  • Device and application information.
  • IP address and network information.
  • Ad impressions, clicks and interaction information.
  • Information used for advertising measurement and fraud prevention.

Advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized, depending on applicable requirements, consent choices and advertising configuration.

Where required, the App provides applicable consent options before relevant advertising data processing. Users may also manage advertising preferences through their device settings and available Google controls.

Learn more:

  • Google Advertising Policies
  • How Google Uses Information from Apps and Websites
  • Google Privacy Policy

04. YouTube Integration

The App provides selected devotional Bhajans and videos through an embedded YouTube player.

When you play YouTube content, YouTube and Google may process information such as device information, playback activity, network information and other technical data according to their own policies.

We do not control YouTube's data collection practices. Please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service

05. Supabase and Online Content

The App uses Supabase-hosted storage to provide devotional content, including wallpapers, Daily Darshan images, Daily Bhakti Status images and other application resources.

When the App requests online content, technical information required to process network requests may be handled by the hosting infrastructure.

Learn more: Supabase Privacy Policy .

06. Offline Storage and App Preferences

Supported devotional audio files may be downloaded and stored locally on your device for offline playback.

Jaap counts, Sankalp progress, reading preferences, favorites and other supported application data may be stored locally to provide app functionality.

Locally stored information may be removed by clearing application data or uninstalling the App. Some downloaded content may also be removed through available app or device controls.

07. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers such as Google Firebase, Google AdMob, YouTube and Supabase when necessary to provide analytics, diagnostics, advertising, video playback and devotional content delivery.

When you choose to share a devotional image or message, the destination application or service you select handles that content according to its own privacy practices. Sharing is initiated by you.

08. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

09. Data Retention

The App does not require a personal user account. Some devotional preferences, downloaded content and progress information may be stored locally on your device.

Information processed by third-party analytics, diagnostic, advertising and hosting providers may be retained according to their respective policies and configured retention periods.

10. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

Third-party services, including analytics and advertising SDKs, may have their own age-related data processing requirements. Applicable audience settings and advertising protections are handled according to the App's Google Play configuration and relevant service requirements.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate action.

11. Third-Party Services

The App integrates or uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Google AdMob for advertising.
  • YouTube embedded video playback.
  • Supabase content storage and delivery.

These services operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review the relevant policies before using their services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in App features, third-party services or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
